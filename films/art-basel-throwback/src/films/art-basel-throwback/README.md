# Art Basel Throwback

This film started as a copy of `example-motion`. It renders as-is, so you always have a working film.
Replace, in this order: theme.ts (brand tokens and fonts), Logo.tsx (official mark), the music
(public/films/<id>/music.wav) with data/beats.json, then the cards and scenes in Film.tsx (storyboard.md).
Compositions: ArtBaselThrowback-<aspect> and ArtBaselThrowback-Cover-<aspect>. Assets: public/films/art-basel-throwback/.
