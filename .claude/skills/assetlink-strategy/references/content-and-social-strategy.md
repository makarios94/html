## The strategy in one table

| | |
|---|---|
| **Job of content** | Make AssetLink known for the kit's tagline, **Know who, why and when**, and turn that into demo requests |
| **Audience** | Growth, recruiting and distribution leaders at ~4,000 wealth firms with $1–20B AUM |
| **Core story** | Filings show a move about 30 days late; AssetLink signals it within about a day, shows why each name fits, and drafts the outreach |
| **Second theme** | Data you can check: customers can verify every detail inside AssetLink. Content shows the data holds up, never where it comes from or how it's built |
| **What's ours** | Movement data from our signals (hotspots, leaderboards, firm growth) and real AssetLink output on screen. Devon's competitor reports note FINTRX, AdvizorPro and ISS publish no open data or benchmarks |
| **Call to action** | A side-by-side on one list, or "let us build a list to your criteria" |

**Standing principle: lead with trust and timing.** "Know who, why and when," backed by data customers can check inside AssetLink (trust) and signals ahead of filings (timing). Every piece of content and messaging should carry at least one of the two.

## How we come up with content: the Content Ideation Wheel [DECISION, Oct 12, 2026]

All AssetLink content ideation follows this five-step loop (after Pierre Herubel's "5-Step Content Ideation Wheel"). It's the direction, not a script: use the concept, adapt the numbers.

1. **Choose content pillars.** Three recurring topics: a broader narrative, a skill or industry focus, and a topic only AssetLink can own.
2. **Ideate sub-ideas.** About 10 per pillar (roughly 30 in total), e.g. "how advisor recruiting has changed," "what to ask before you renew a data contract."
3. **Try formats.** Pick from a menu such as carousel, infographic, comparison table, problem–agitate–solve, story, before vs. after, build in public, team photo. Match each sub-idea to a format, and test several.
4. **Publish and engage.** A steady rhythm with engagement around each post (the wheel suggests about 15 minutes before and after).
5. **Analyze and reuse.** Track impressions, engagement, save rate, followers and profile visits. Reuse the best ideas and formats, and repurpose winners (e.g. a webinar into clips, a report into data posts).

**Decided with the user, never alone:** which formats we use, and how often we publish. Propose options with a recommendation, then wait for the user's call. The wheel's "8 formats" and "5 posts a week for 4 weeks" are reference points only.

**Fit with this plan:** the pillars sit under the core story ("Know who, why and when"), and every sub-idea still passes the guardrails below, especially no sources or methods (§9.2 of `assetlink-context.md`) and live capabilities only. The two series (*The Asset Link* newsletter, *Advisor Movement Report*) and the LinkedIn page mix are where the wheel's posts land. The wheel is internal working method. It stays out of the leadership pages.

**Topics and questions (Oct 13, 2026, the user's direction):** start from 12 content topics drawn from the positioning, find about 6 real questions buyers ask under each. **Don't base questions on Google search or search intent** [DECISION, Oct 13, 2026]: in this space it's dominated by consumers (B2C), not our B2B buyers. Use B2B sources instead: questions from sales calls and demos, Devon's qualifying questions and objections, customer conversations, trade press, conference agendas, research firms, and what buyer titles discuss on LinkedIn, and build each piece of content as an answer to one question, with a format assigned to each. Current plan: the "Content Topics & Questions" page in `CLAUDE.md`. The 12 topics, split 7 / 3 / 2 to match the ~60/25/15 use-case weighting: recruiting (recruiting advisors · why advisors move, and when · reaching advisors · what a recruit is worth · advisor movement trends · timing: getting there first · advisor data you can trust), selling (selling to advisors · reaching RIAs as an asset manager · who to call and when), acquiring (buying RIAs and wealth firms · which firms are growing, shrinking or in play). Every angle must come back to who/why/when and to timing or trust; general industry advice alone doesn't qualify. Questions are written in the buyer's own words (leaders, not reps or advisors), and each row shows who asks it and where it comes from (Devon's ICP, Messaging Kit, competitor reports, trade press, AssetLink data). Each piece names its home: LinkedIn, *The Asset Link* newsletter, Movement Report, website or webinar, with one live webinar question in each selling and acquiring topic. No AI-as-the-headline topic: AI shows up only as "AI drafts, people decide." Never pick a question whose honest answer would give out the secret sauce (e.g. "where does advisor data come from?").

## Signature series

**Start with two:** *The Asset Link* newsletter and the *Advisor Movement Report*. Add *Search Walkthrough* only once those two run steadily (earliest month 3).

| Series | Format and cadence |
|---|---|
| ***The Asset Link*** | Our existing LinkedIn newsletter, every other Thursday (description, updated Oct 19, 2026: "Bi-weekly insights and actionable data for wealth firms, recruiting leaders and asset managers." (95 characters; the original 109-character line fit LinkedIn's space)): notable advisor moves and firm changes, one tip, one data point. Always use this name; never invent a new newsletter name [DECISION, Oct 19, 2026] |
| ***Advisor Movement Report*** | Monthly data report: hiring hotspots, growing and shrinking firms, team moves. Pitched to trade press |
| ***Search Walkthrough*** | Video every two weeks: an ideal advisor profile goes in; a matched list, signals and a drafted first message come out. Results only, never what happens behind the screen |

Walkthroughs use composite or anonymized profiles, never a real advisor's name, face or contact details.

## Channels

**Main three:** the newsletter, *The Asset Link* on LinkedIn (a subscriber audience) · the AssetLink LinkedIn page (the most active channel) · the website (search pages built over time, e.g. "advisor recruiting data").

**On the LinkedIn page:**
- The monthly report.
- The founder's posts (optional): shared when the founder has time; the page never depends on them.
- Thought leadership: points of view on recruiting, selling to advisors and firm consolidation.
- Data and stats posts: one chart or number from our own data, labeled as AssetLink data. Never say where the data comes from or how it's built.
- Pain points and how AssetLink solves them, one post per buyer problem: recruiting (stale contacts, late moves, too few people → signals within a day, contacts, drafted emails); distribution (hours in data packs → lists by firm, market and allocation, team data); corporate development (can't see who's growing → leaderboards, hotspots, fast-growing firms).
- Live webinar events: a monthly live webinar with a functional guest speaker (not necessarily famous) who reaches our buyers and can become a partner: recruiting leaders, heads of distribution, RIA founders, transition consultants, M&A advisors, CRM and wealth-tech partners, association or podcast hosts. Each becomes several clips, and the guest shares it with their network. First one in month 2. Speakers speak for themselves: no scripted endorsements, and written approval before featuring a speaker from a customer firm.

**Add later:** founder and leadership LinkedIn as a regular voice, trade press pitches from the report (AdvisorHub, InvestmentNews, WealthManagement.com, Financial Planning, ThinkAdvisor), industry conferences, and paid LinkedIn aimed at firms nearing a data-contract renewal.

## Timing content to buying triggers (from the ICP)

- Data contracts up for renewal (FINTRX, AdvizorPro, MarketPro): "what to ask before you renew"
- A firm opening a new market or office: market reports for that city
- Industry events that free advisors: publish the data within days
- Acquisition pushes: firm-growth data and fast-growing firm lists

## 90-day shape

1. **Month 1, get the idea out:** launch post (founder if available, otherwise the company page), first newsletter, first movement report, the pilot story (once approved).
2. **Month 2, show how it works:** pain-point posts for each buyer, the first live webinar, first press pitch from the report, a one-page sales sheet.
3. **Month 3, show it works for customers:** a customer story, a live session at an event, paid ads to firms nearing renewal. If the two series are steady, add the first Search Walkthrough.

**Before launch:** update the website and decks with the kit's tagline and one-liner, and the confirmed numbers.

## Guardrails

1. Only Product Reality "live" or "managed service" capabilities; nothing in development.
2. The kit's words: "finds advisors who match your criteria" (not "ranks"), "signals who may be moving" (not "predicts"), "decision intelligence" (not "AI-powered" everything), "in contracting" (not "signed").
3. No individual advisors in public content. Firm-level trends need leadership approval.
4. Pilot figures only with their context.
5. No competitor names in public, except data contracts in renewal content Devon approves.
6. No customer names without permission: "customers across insurance, asset management and wealth management."
7. No methodology beyond the kit's boilerplate.
8. Plain words, short sentences.

## Measurement

Set baselines in month 1. Track newsletter sign-ups from target titles, engagement from those leaders, press pickups of the movement report, content-sourced demo requests, and firms sharing their ideal advisor profile. Review monthly, and cut any series that doesn't move after 8 weeks.

## Unlocks later

- Self-serve plans launch → Starter-lane content with clear prices (all three main competitors hide theirs).
