# Positioning & Messaging

**Source of truth: Devon's *AssetLink Messaging and Marketing Kit* (October 8, 2026).** Every line in it matches the Product Reality doc. Use it for the website, decks, posts and outreach, and check any new claim against Product Reality before adding it. The full leadership version (v5) is the private page listed in the repo's `CLAUDE.md`. This file is the working summary. Pricing, customer names and pilot figures stay in the internal documents.

## Core lines (use exactly)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Tagline** | Know who, why and when. |
| **One-liner** | AssetLink is a decision intelligence platform for wealth management that finds the right advisors and firms for your criteria, shows why they fit, and signals when to act. |
| **Boilerplate (~50 words)** | AssetLink helps wealth firms, asset managers and fintechs decide who to recruit, sell to or acquire. It combines regulatory filings, advisor websites, social activity and news with each client's own criteria and outcomes, then drafts the outreach. Customers span insurance, asset management and wealth management. |

**Category:** *decision intelligence* (per the kit). An earlier internal proposal to switch to "relationship intelligence" is retired.

**Spine:** "compete on timing" is backed by *who* (lists built to the client's own profile), *why* (the reason each name fits, and the source of every field), and *when* (signals from news in about a day, versus about 30 days for filings).

**Standing principle: lead with trust and timing.** "Know who, why and when," backed by a source for every field (trust) and signals ahead of filings (timing). Every piece of content and messaging should carry at least one of the two.

## Message by buyer (from the kit)

| Buyer | Lead with | Proof to use |
|---|---|---|
| Head of Advisor Recruiting | "Find advisors who fit your profile, and reach them when they're ready to move." | Signals ahead of filings, drafted outreach, a private view of your own hires |
| Chief Growth Officer | "One recruit pays for AssetLink for seven years." | The recruiting math and the time you get back |
| Head of Distribution or Sales Enablement | "Put your product in front of the advisors most likely to need it." | Filters by firm, market and allocation, plus team data |
| Corporate Development | "See which RIAs and wealth firms are growing, shrinking or in play." | Leaderboards, hotspots and fast-growing firm lists |

Recruiting is the lead use (the fastest growing, per the ICP).

## Differentiators (live, per Product Reality)

1. **Signals ahead of filings:** news-based change detection in about a day, plus leaderboards, hotspots and firm growth.
2. **Why each name fits, and where the data came from:** lists built to the client's ideal profile; every field traces to its source (filing or web page). Competitors make accuracy claims without showing sources.
3. **Outreach done for you:** drafted emails into Outlook/Gmail; nothing sends automatically. Managed-service scoring against the client's profile.
4. **Private by design:** a client's profile, lists and outcomes stay in their own instance and never train other clients' models.
5. **Easy start:** managed delivery for security-cautious firms (we send output, they integrate nothing).

Proof: the managed-service scoring pilot and customer feedback on contact data (figures in the internal docs; always state pilot context).

## Outreach templates (from the kit)

Lead with a real signal about the prospect's firm, then one question. Under 90 words each. The templates cover three buyers: recruiting leaders, distribution leaders and corporate development.

## Demo flow (from the kit)

1. Ask what a good advisor looks like for them, and set it as their profile.
2. Show a list built to that profile, with the fields they care about.
3. Open one advisor: contact data, team, activity and where it came from.
4. Show a signal: a leaderboard, hotspot or recent change.
5. Draft an outreach email into the drafts folder and agree the next step.

**Avoid in live demos:** free-form agent questions on new topics, sorting, and cities or firm names you haven't checked.

## Objection answers (from the kit)

| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| "We already have a data vendor." | Run a side-by-side on one list. Customers tell us the contact data is the difference |
| "I worry about another tool." | Start with a small test group and your own criteria; use the platform or push drafts into existing tools |
| "Will our data train other clients' models?" | No. It stays in your private instance |
| "Security won't allow third-party access." | Start with managed delivery, then move to platform access |
| "How do I know the data is right?" | Every field traces to its source, so you can audit it |

## Words to use and avoid (from the kit and Product Reality)

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Finds advisors who match your criteria | Ranks advisors |
| Signals who may be moving | Predicts who will move |
| Generally available and actively developed | MVP |
| Decision intelligence | AI-powered everything |
| In contracting | Signed, until it is |
| Customers across insurance, asset management and wealth management | Naming customers without permission |
| On the roadmap | Always-on agents (still being built) |

Never mention estimated individual AUM, or describe anything in development as shipped.
